Task Hover Preview · Compass Check

When a task title isn't enough — hover (or long-press on iOS) to see why it's there. The preview surfaces just enough context to make a confident decision without leaving the step.

Design Reasoning

The Compass Check is a quick noon-to-noon ritual. Pulling up a full task detail breaks that flow. A hover preview is the right pattern: it appears while your eye is on the row, disappears the moment you move on. The preview's job is to answer one question — "do I remember what this is, and is it still worth my attention?"

  • What to show (in priority order): full title (in case truncated), notes, last activity, quadrant + state + due date, tags, created date
  • How to show it: a small popover anchored to the row, arrow on the side, fixed width (~340 px), no scroll inside
  • What it must not do: cover the row it describes, block keyboard nav, or require a click to dismiss

Tweak the controls (top-right) to compare content density and visual styles.
